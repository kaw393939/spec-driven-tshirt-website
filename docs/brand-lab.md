# The shirt stays the same.

Change the story and composition. What changes about your impression?

These are **illustrative sketches**, not completed brands or templates you must use. Archetype and visual approach are independent choices. The modernist/postmodernist labels are starting points for discussion, not complete definitions.

{% include brand-lab.html %}

## Look, then explain

- What did the language, type, color and arrangement make you expect?
- Which choice made the biggest difference? Why might it fit a particular audience?
- Can the same archetype support both compositions? What would you revise?

The choices here are simplified examples. Both styles must retain readable text and usable controls. The actual shirt, sizes and product facts have not changed.

> **AI aside · Use this as a question, not an answer.** Tell me what you want the audience to feel, then ask for alternatives. You still choose and justify your own direction.
